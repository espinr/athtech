AthTech, the Athletics Technology Conference, is a meeting place for all the people working behind the scenes to make the sport more enjoyable and easier to manage.

Previous editions focused on standardisation and data flows to help countries digitize the sport and save time, and on how all the data and content can be used, to offer great user experiences to athletes and fans or to improve the economics of the sport.

Come and hear from World Athletics and European Athletics, from member federations, race organisers and service providers, to be at the cutting edge of the sport!

This conference is open to anyone interested in any topic related to technology and sports. from anywhere in the world

Read what happened in in 2017 (Portugal), 2018 (Spain), 2021 (Croatia), 2023 (Malta), and 2025 (San Marino).
